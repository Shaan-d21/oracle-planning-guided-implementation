# Phase 13 — Workforce & CapEx Dependencies practice pack

Use these files in `Plan1`, `Forecast`, `Working` for the ApexPlan dependency lab. Workforce and CapEx are summarized by existing Entity, Account, and Month structures. Hours, FTE, people, utilization, lead time, and asset counts use No Currency; overtime, compensation, investment, payments, and depreciation are planned in INR for Pune and Noida and translated to USD only for governed reporting. Do not create Employee, Job, Position, Project, or Asset dimensions unless a formal design change is approved.

## Controlled flow

1. Freeze approved plant starts, rates, required hours, cost versions, and exceptions.
2. Calculate productive hours, exact FTE, roster FTE, current capacity, and local gaps.
3. Compare overtime, transfer, contractor, training, hiring, and production-rebalance actions.
4. Apply feasible operational mitigations before sizing CapEx.
5. Verify asset lead time and enabling workforce, facility, utility, and maintenance dependencies.
6. Plan investment, payment cash flow, capitalization, in-service timing, and depreciation separately.
7. Approve one scenario and reconcile affected operational, cost, reporting, and financial results.
