# Phase 11 — Inventory Planning practice pack

Use these files to complete the ApexPlan inventory-planning lab. Work in `Plan1`, `Forecast`, `Working`, Jan FY27 unless the test case states otherwise. The planning grain is Product × Entity × Month. Pune and Noida are Entity members; do not create a Warehouse dimension.

## Controlled flow

1. Reconcile book inventory to usable opening inventory.
2. Approve Product-level policy inputs and effective dates.
3. Calculate cycle stock, safety stock, and the pack-rounded target.
4. Project ending inventory, target variance, and days cover.
5. Balance Pune and Noida with a feasible transfer.
6. Resolve exceptions and rerun affected Phase 10 production.
7. Reconcile Plan1 totals and the ApexPlan reporting aggregate.
