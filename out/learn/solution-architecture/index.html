Lesson 01 · 10 min

Architecture foundations

In progress
Architecture objectiveMake major solution decisions explicit before detailed application design and configuration.

Solution architecture translates approved requirements into boundaries, capabilities, flows, controls, and measurable quality targets. It explains why the solution is shaped a certain way and who owns each dependency. It is not a product-feature list or a polished diagram with unresolved decisions hidden behind it.

Business-led

Every component and interface traces to an approved process, requirement, control, or quality need.

Governed data

Sources of record, grain, mappings, validation, reconciliation, recovery, and ownership are explicit.

Secure by design

Identity, least privilege, segregation of duties, service access, and evidence are part of the blueprint.

Operable by design

Performance, availability, release, rollback, monitoring, support, and continuity are testable.
