Discovery foundations

In progress
Why Discovery existsCreate a shared, evidence-seeking understanding of the business before proposing an Oracle Planning design.

Discovery is the first controlled project phase. The team learns which decisions the client needs to improve, how planning works, who owns it, what data and controls exist, and what success means. It reduces the risk of building technically correct screens that solve the wrong business problem.

Outcome-led

Start with decisions, measurable outcomes, and pain—not a list of screens.

Cross-functional

Include business owners, planners, operations, finance, and technology.

Evidence-seeking

Treat stakeholder statements as inputs to validate, not automatic facts.

Controlled output

Record owners, assumptions, decisions, open questions, and approvals.

Discovery doesUnderstand, scope, question, collect, document, prioritize, and obtain agreement.
Discovery does notConfigure dimensions, promise a solution, or confirm root causes without evidence.
