Production planning foundations and controls

In progress
Business outcomeCreate a feasible, explainable production recommendation from approved demand without hiding inventory, yield, lot, plant, or capacity assumptions.

Production planning is a connected supply decision, not a demand copy. The model converts required good units into manufacturing starts, respects lot sizes and Product-to-plant capability, allocates work to Pune and Noida, exposes overloads, and preserves every assumption for review.
