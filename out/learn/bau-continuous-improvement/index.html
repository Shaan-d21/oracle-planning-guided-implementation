BAU foundations and service ownership

In progress
A product and service—not a finished projectBAU protects the live planning service while continuous improvement changes it through transparent value, risk, architecture, release, adoption, and benefit governance.

Project closure transfers accountability; it does not freeze the application. Business and technology owners must operate each planning cycle, maintain controls and knowledge, respond to platform and business change, prioritize investments, and prove that improvements deliver sustainable outcomes.

ImproveGoverned product roadmapEvidence-led intake, prioritization, design, release, adoption, and benefit measurement
