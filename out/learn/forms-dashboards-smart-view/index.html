User-experience strategy and role journeys

In progress
Design around work, not artifactsGive each role the shortest clear path from owned input to validated decision, while keeping context, security, calculations, and evidence visible.

A good Oracle Planning experience does not reproduce an uncontrolled spreadsheet. It guides users through approved tasks, protects calculated results, explains exceptions, and uses Smart View as another governed interface to the same Planning data.

NavigationShow the role's next task and relevant artifacts.
Plan1 formsCollect owned inputs and review detailed calculations.
Phase 15 actionsValidate, calculate, move, and record job evidence.
ApexPlan dashboardsPresent reconciled decisions and exceptions.
Smart ViewSupport governed connected analysis and limited input.
