AS-IS assessment foundations

In progress
Assessment objectiveCreate an agreed, evidence-backed understanding of how planning decisions are made today.

Discovery established the business objective, scope, stakeholders, and initial requirements. Phase 2 now tests the hypotheses: follow real transactions and planning cycles, inspect representative files and logs, measure effort and quality, and identify the control gaps the future state must address.

Phase 01 · Discovery asksWhat does the business need and why?

Defines objectives, scope, requirement themes, owners, and evidence requests.

Phase 02 · Assessment provesHow does the work actually happen today?

Validates process, handoffs, systems, data, rules, controls, pain, and measurable performance.

Phase 03 · Design decidesHow should the connected future process work?

Uses agreed findings and constraints to design the target operating model.
