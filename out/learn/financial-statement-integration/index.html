Financial integration foundations and boundaries

In progress
Business outcomeProduce one financially coherent management outlook in which profit, working capital, cash, assets, liabilities, and equity all respond to the same approved operating assumptions.

Financial integration is the control layer that proves operational decisions have been valued and classified consistently. It does not replace a general ledger, statutory consolidation, accounting close, eliminations, journals, or external reporting.
