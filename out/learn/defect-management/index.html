Defect management foundations

In progress
Release-quality governanceA defect is an evidenced gap between approved expected behavior and observed behavior in an identifiable release—not every question, request, or preference.

Phase 22 consolidates issues from SIT, performance testing, and UAT into one controlled release view. The team must protect requirement and test traceability, distinguish impact severity from scheduling priority, manage fixes across environments, and prevent cutover from inheriting unknown or unauthorized risk.

ControlAuthoritative defect processClassification, triage, ownership, SLA, fixes, retest, regression, and reconciliation
OutputCutover-ready positionNo blocker plus explicit ownership and authorization for every accepted residual risk
