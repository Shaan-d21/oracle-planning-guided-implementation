# Phase 12 — Manufacturing Cost & COGS practice pack

Use these files to complete the ApexPlan costing lab in `Plan1`, `Forecast`, `Working`, Jan FY27. Detailed cost is calculated at Product × Entity × Month in INR and moved through the approved mapping to the `ApexPlan` ASO reporting cube. Quantities, hours, utilization, and yields use No Currency; monetary rates, unit cost, inventory value, COGS, and margin use INR locally and translate to USD only through the governed reporting process.

## Controlled flow

1. Freeze approved production, inventory, demand, revenue, and cost versions.
2. Validate BOM quantities, effective rates, units, and normal-loss treatment.
3. Validate labor, machine, variable-overhead, and fixed-overhead drivers.
4. Calculate and independently reproduce the manufacturing unit cost.
5. Value beginning inventory and expected good output.
6. Calculate weighted-average cost, COGS, ending inventory, and gross margin.
7. Resolve exceptions, rerun changed operating scopes, and reconcile reporting.
