# Phase 24 — Go / No-Go practice pack

Use these templates to convert the completed cutover evidence into one controlled production-activation decision. The decision must apply to an exact release and evidence snapshot. A failed hard-stop gate cannot be overridden by an aggregate readiness score.

## Files

- `go-no-go-gate-register.csv` — acceptance rules, evidence, results, reviewers, and gate recommendations.
- `residual-risk-register.csv` — impact, workaround, condition, monitoring, breach action, and risk acceptance.
- `decision-record.csv` — authority, quorum, outcome, rationale, activation scope, recovery state, and signatures.
- `communication-checklist.csv` — approved leadership, user, operations, support, and recovery communications.

Replace sample owners, dates, thresholds, and references with approved project values. Do not store credentials, secrets, or sensitive production data in this pack.
